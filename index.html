FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

The European Accessibility Act (EAA) is an EU directive requiring websites and digital services to meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards. It covers e-commerce, banking, media, transport, and telecom sectors — if you serve EU customers in these areas, it applies to you. The compliance deadline passed June 28, 2025, and enforcement is now active in several member states.

Non-compliant businesses face fines up to €50,000 per violation, legal challenges from affected users or advocacy groups, and mandatory remediation orders from national regulators. Germany and France have already begun active audit campaigns targeting e-commerce sites. Reputational damage from public legal challenges can significantly exceed the fines themselves.

We use axe-core — the industry standard trusted by Microsoft, Google, and thousands of developers worldwide — which automatically catches approximately 57% of WCAG 2.1 AA violations. Our Pro plan's AI Fix Guide (powered by Claude) addresses the nuanced manual-check items that automated tools can't fully evaluate, bridging the gap to near-complete coverage.

Start with Critical violations — these are highest-risk for regulatory action. Use the AI-generated before/after code fixes in your PDF to implement changes directly, or forward the report to your development team. Each violation links to the relevant WCAG success criterion so your team understands the requirement, not just the fix. For ongoing protection and client reporting, consider the Agency plan.
